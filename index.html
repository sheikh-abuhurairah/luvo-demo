How it works

Design, print, deliver — nothing outsourced

One studio handles your entire order, so what you approve on screen is exactly what lands at your door.

01 — Design

Send your logo

Share your brand name, colours and any reference photos. We lay it out on the exact product you're ordering, not a generic template.

02 — Proof

Approve the mockup

You see a proof before anything is cut or printed. Unlimited tweaks until the placement, size and finish feel right.

03 — Print & Ship

Made and packed by hand

Every batch is cut, punched or woven in-house, then packed flat or on spools and shipped anywhere — local or international.
